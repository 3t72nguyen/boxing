    Footwork for each punch

    • Ljab
      Left jabStep in with the front foot and let the back foot slide after it. Keep your feet shoulder width apart.
    • Rcross
      Right crossPush off the back toes, turn the back heel out and rotate your hips. Step in as you throw to add power.
    • Lhook
      Left hookPivot on the ball of the front foot. Hips and shoulders turn together, elbow level with the fist.
    • Rhook
      Right hookPivot on the ball of the back foot and let the hips drive the punch. Keep the front knee soft.
    • Lupper
      Left uppercutDip your knees and sink slightly left, then drive up through the legs.
    • Rupper
      Right uppercutSink onto the back leg, then drive up and turn the hips. Keep it short and tight.

    Step in: front foot first, back foot follows. Step back: back foot first, front foot follows. Never cross your feet. Pivot left or right: plant the ball of one foot and swing the other foot around it about 45 degrees. Written for an orthodox stance (left foot forward). If you fight southpaw, swap L and R.
